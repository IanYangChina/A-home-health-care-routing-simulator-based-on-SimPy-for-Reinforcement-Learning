Findings

On D, the ACO mix raised DQN held-out reward from 296.78 to 486.93. The short-wait warm start raised PPO from −104.58 to 58.71. On A, both DQN guides lowered held-out reward and both PPO warm starts yielded small gains. All learned variants scored below Random and ACO on both layouts. For the DQN ACO mix on D, weighted training reward fell from 1,895.35 to 534.31 as waiting rose from 693 to 2,447 nurse-minutes across the plotted windows.
