Agent work

Compared stronger waiting penalties and longer return horizons as separate changes. DQN used 16- and 32-decision replay targets with the tuned discount. PPO used λ=1 with its tuned discount and a separate undiscounted λ=1 arm. Every PPO arm used 512-decision rollouts. A rollout ending mid-shift still bootstrapped. All arms treated the modeled shift end as terminal for learning.

Computation

Ran 60 fits across C and D at 12,288 steps each, with 3 training seeds and 12 held-out seeds per learned arm. Collected 4,233 complete training episodes and 720 learned-policy evaluations. Reused 48 saved Random and ACO baseline evaluations. The route pages use training seed 11.

Findings

On C, DQN 32-step had the highest reference reward among the learned arms at 534.94. Random scored 187.97 and ACO scored 851.16.

C DQN. Of the two stronger penalties, 0.30 points per minute yielded the lower mean waiting. Compared with control, it moved waiting from 1,210.15 to 1,263.43 nurse-minutes and completed visits from 73.22 to 71.25. The better of the two longer-return arms, DQN 32-step, moved reference reward from −21.32 to 534.94 and completed visits from 73.22 to 102.08.

C PPO. Of the two stronger penalties, 0.30 points per minute yielded the lower mean waiting. Compared with control, it moved waiting from 1,245.35 to 1,219.80 nurse-minutes and completed visits from 71.58 to 72.53. The better of the two longer-return arms, PPO λ=1, moved reference reward from −52.23 to −75.48 and completed visits from 71.58 to 70.36.

On D, DQN 16-step had the highest reference reward among the learned arms at 725.46. Random scored 844.28 and ACO scored 2,456.45.

D DQN. Of the two stronger penalties, 0.15 points per minute yielded the lower mean waiting. Compared with control, it moved waiting from 2,701.65 to 2,660.41 nurse-minutes and completed visits from 159.50 to 160.97. The better of the two longer-return arms, DQN 16-step, moved reference reward from 296.78 to 725.46 and completed visits from 159.50 to 181.97.

D PPO. Of the two stronger penalties, 0.30 points per minute yielded the lower mean waiting. Compared with control, it moved waiting from 3,027.45 to 3,000.29 nurse-minutes and completed visits from 141.14 to 134.53. The better of the two longer-return arms, PPO λ=1, moved reference reward from −48.94 to 25.80 and completed visits from 141.14 to 145.14.

A replay of held-out shifts from the predeclared seed-11 checkpoints found that D PPO with a 0.30 waiting penalty deferred on 47.3% of decisions with at least one feasible visit. D PPO control and λ=1, and D DQN 16- and 32-step, used no voluntary deferrals in these replays.

On D, PPO λ=1 and γ=1 had a mean reference reward of −790.30. The seed-13 checkpoint scored −2,372.96.
