Agent work

Verified the installed SB3 defaults and reconciled them with the Round 9 configuration. Used the same full nurse roster, guided exploration and waiting cost. The attention variant appends two 16-dimensional summaries to the same flattened inputs. Return horizon calibration

MaskablePPO defaults to a 2,048-decision rollout and λ = 0.95. Round 9 explicitly used 512 decisions and λ = 1. Its tested values 0.998 and 0.9995 were discounts γ. A fixed rollout controls collection and update cadence. λ controls how strongly later residuals contribute. MaskablePPO parameter reference.

The nominal PPO horizon is the infinite geometric weight sum 1 / (1 − γλ) for future TD residuals, so λ = (1 − 1 / horizon) / γ. PPO uses all residuals up to the rollout or shift boundary, with an estimated continuation at a nonterminal rollout boundary. DQN directly uses up to N rewards, discounted at its own γ, and estimates the continuation from its target network. These two horizon measures are on a common decision scale and describe different targets.
